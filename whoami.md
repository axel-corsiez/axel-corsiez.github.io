Offensive security. Mostly vulnerability research on open-source software: reading code,
building the tooling that makes reading code at scale possible, and reporting what comes
out of it to the people who maintain it.

Areas I spend time in:

- **Vulnerability research.** Source-code auditing of open-source projects, variant
  analysis on published fixes, coordinated disclosure.
- **Web application security.** Access control, server-side request forgery, injection,
  authentication and session handling.
- **Offensive tooling.** Research pipelines, automation around static analysis, and the
  glue that turns a corpus of repositories into a ranked shortlist.

Everything published here concerns systems I am authorised to test or open-source code
analysed locally. Findings are reported to maintainers first and written about only once
they are fixed.
